## The Role of AI in Modern Cyber Security

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is becoming an important part of modern cyber security. As cyber attacks become more frequent and sophisticated, security teams need technologies that can process large amounts of security data and identify potential threats quickly.

AI can help Security Operations Centers (SOCs) analyze logs, network traffic, endpoint events, and threat intelligence. Machine learning can identify unusual patterns and assist analysts in detecting potentially malicious activities.

AI can also support phishing detection, malware analysis, vulnerability prioritization, threat intelligence, fraud detection, and automated incident response. For example, an AI-powered SOC platform can correlate multiple security events, identify suspicious behavior, map activity to frameworks such as MITRE ATT&CK, and provide useful investigation context.

However, AI is not a complete replacement for cybersecurity professionals. AI systems can produce false positives or incorrect conclusions, while attackers can also use AI to develop more sophisticated attacks. Human expertise and validation therefore remain essential.

The future of cybersecurity will increasingly involve collaboration between security professionals and AI-powered tools. AI can reduce repetitive tasks, improve detection speed, and allow analysts to focus on complex investigations and decision-making.

Through the Jagspire Cyber Security Internship, I am building practical knowledge in Python, networking, log analysis, APIs, and security tools. These fundamentals will help me understand how AI can be applied to real-world cybersecurity challenges.
